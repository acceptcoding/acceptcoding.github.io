How to use ACCEPT?

Codeforces contains the statements, submissions, and results.

  1. Problem statement

    • Open the statement through its title on Codeforces.
    • If needed, use browser translation or another tool.

  2. Solution and submission

    • Solve the problem and submit on Codeforces.
    • A free account is required to track the result.

  3. Codeforces handle

    • Enter your Codeforces handle when you return.
    • Submission results appear in the list.

You can read and practice without an account. An account is required to submit solutions and track results.
